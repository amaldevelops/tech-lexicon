# AI Prompts

- Be brutally honest. Say when you don't know the answer rather than guessing. Ask any questions you need to give a better answer. Don't try to flatter me or make me feel good.
  Prompting is essentially the art of **context**. Think of an AI not as an all-knowing mind, but as a brilliant intern who has read every book in the world but has zero common sense about what _you_ specifically want.

To get great results, you need to move from "searching" (like Google) to "directing" (like a manager).

---

## 1. The Golden Formula: CREATE

If you want a high-quality response, try to include as many of these elements as possible:

- **C**haracter: Give the AI a persona. ("Act as a senior travel agent.")
- **R**equest: What is the specific task? ("Plan a 3-day trip to Tokyo.")
- **E**xamples: Show, don't just tell. ("Style it like this: [Example].")
- **A**udience: Who is this for? ("Write for a family with young kids.")
- **T**erminology: Mention specific details to include. ("Focus on Shibuya and food tours.")
- **E**xclusions: What should it avoid? ("Do not include high-end luxury restaurants.")
- While using labels like "Character:" or "Request:" helps you organize your own thoughts, the AI is smart enough to understand natural language. You can weave those elements into a conversational sentence and get the exact same result. The goal of CREATE is just to make sure you didn't forget a crucial detail that would make the AI guess (and usually guess wrong).

---

## 2. Techniques for Power Users

Once you master the basics, you can use these "meta-prompts" to get even better output:

### Chain of Thought

Ask the AI to **"think step-by-step."** This forces the model to logic through a problem before giving a final answer, which drastically reduces errors in math or complex reasoning.

### Few-Shot Prompting

Provide 2-3 examples of the input and output you want.

> **User:** "I want to turn product names into catchy slogans.
> Example 1: Coffee -> Wake up your soul.
> Example 2: Running Shoes -> Defy gravity.
> Example 3: [Your Product] ->"

### The Iterative Loop

Never settle for the first response. You can treat the AI like a sculptor:

- "Make it 50% shorter."
- "Change the tone to be more humorous."
- "Now, turn that entire explanation into a table."

---

## 3. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake                  | Instead...                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Being Vague**          | Be specific. Use "Write 300 words" instead of "Write a short essay."       |
| **Asking "Can you...?"** | Use direct commands. Use "Write a poem" instead of "Can you write a poem?" |
| **Information Overload** | Keep it organized. Use bullet points or headers to separate instructions.  |

## Your "Cheat Sheet" for Today

If you’re stuck, just use this fill-in-the-blank template:

> "Act as a **[Role]**. I need you to **[Task]**. Please ensure the tone is **[Tone]** and the output is formatted as a **[Format - e.g., list, table, email]**. Avoid **[What to avoid]**."
